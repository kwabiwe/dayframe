# Location Learning Guardrails

Use this when changing Expo background sampling, learned-place events, `learned_places`, or location Review UI.

## Location Intelligence V2 architecture

`packages/shared/src/location/` owns the deterministic `location-v2.0` evidence, matching, segmentation, commute, DTO, and fixture contracts. Thresholds live only in `LOCATION_ENGINE_V2_CONFIG`. Both the account-isolated mobile SQLite journal and the server replay service call the same pure engine. V1 learned-cluster classification below applies only to explicit `v1` mode; do not mix its recurrence counters into V2 temporal continuity.

Finalisation is time-driven as well as evidence-driven. After the ten-minute lag, mobile must re-run its local journal with current time and call the authenticated retained-evidence replay route even when there is no new upload. Evidence ingest commits the event summary and evidence without semantic replay. Explicit replay has a separate bounded transaction under the same owner advisory lock and owns segmentation, the rollout acknowledgement/cutover check, semantic emission, and idempotency. An upload acknowledgement proves evidence durability only; replay failure must remain retryable even after the upload queue is empty.

Evidence sources are Expo standard locations and geofences plus the local `dayframe-location-visits` module's `CLVisit`, significant-change, provider, pause, and resume signals. Native callbacks persist a bounded protected Application Support queue and perform no networking. JavaScript clears a native signal only after its durable SQLite acceptance or deliberate ownership/validation discard commits. The complete saved/accepted-learned place catalogue is passed to matching even though iOS registers no more than 20 geofence regions.

Bootstrap must consult the existing logout/session fence before mobile-account activation, including during final account-removal I/O. Keep explicit consent intent separate from obsolete capture work: an authorised owner-scoped off write survives logout/401 in the lifecycle lane, and a later authorised on write wins. A disabled binding cannot be enabled by bootstrap after failed preference persistence. Durable off must attempt OS teardown even when a subsequent SQLite step fails; off/disabled callbacks and bootstrap reconcile monitoring without deleting accepted work or semantic eligibility. Serialise those stops before replacement starts and count/report OS failures. Immediate OS removal after process termination or OS failure remains unverified.

Failed logout recovery separates active API request gating from unresolved Location cleanup: only fresh still-current bootstraps recover after failure; earlier invalidated responses stay stale and capture stays closed. Filling a legacy owner without replacing the token/generation cannot release cleanup. Stored consent and effective capture are separate Settings diagnostics. Inactive activation exposes explicit Retry capture via the existing enable path; bootstrap cannot repair it by granting capture from stored true. Recheck capture/consent after source-start awaits before reporting on, and refresh effective state after failure.

Initial capture profile:

- `distanceInterval: 75m`; this is a movement filter, never a dwell timer.
- deferred delivery after `200m` or `300s`, timeout `300s`.
- `pausesUpdatesAutomatically: false`, activity type `Other`.
- no iOS `timeInterval` assumption.
- maximum accepted horizontal accuracy `200m`; matching allowance capped at `60m`.
- saved dwell `5m`; unanchored candidate dwell `10m` with at least three samples; unknown review threshold `20m`.
- continuity gap `12m`; finalisation lag `10m`; two corroborating outside samples.
- a quiet reporting gap may bridge two parts of the same unknown stay for at
  most `60m` when their centres remain within `120m` and no intervening route or
  contradictory-place evidence exists; this is continuity, not a second visit.
- journal/server evidence expiry `7d`; local frozen upload bodies and retained engine/account/segment context do not share the journal expiry; upload batches at most 100 items.

Temporal invariants:

- Evidence is ordered by occurrence time, source precedence, and stable client ID; duplicate delivery is idempotent.
- A later appearance at the same coordinates is not continuity. `A -> B -> A` is three stays.
- An accepted different saved/learned place closes the current stay. A single noisy outside point does not; two corroborating outside points can.
- A completed `CLVisit` can support a gap, but accepted intervening-place evidence breaks that support.
- A completed Visit departure at or after the latest inside observation and at or before the transition is the exact stay stop and commute start, including both evidence bounds. Do not replace it with a midpoint to the first moving sample. Earlier contradictory-place evidence or later inside evidence invalidates that departure for the transition; keep the ordinary bounded estimate in those cases. Exceptions: a corroborated arrival's presence ends the stay at the earliest departure evidence, and without one a completed saved-place Visit ending no more than `savedPlaceVisitDepartureLagMaximumMs` (five minutes) after the earliest departure evidence ends the stay at that evidence (see corroborated arrival presence below).
- Initial geofence state is not an arrival. A bare geofence exit yields bounded uncertainty rather than a fabricated precise departure.
- Boundaries retain lower/upper evidence bounds. Manual corrections use `continuity_status = manual` and canonical replay must not overwrite them.
- Commutes represent meaningful travel between contiguous stays, not every
  movement between stationary clusters. Distinct endpoints normally need at
  least `800m` separation, or at least `1200m` of efficient route evidence
  outside the `450m` local-movement band. A distant arrival with no departure
  or route observation is not a commute. Speed is corroboration only: at least
  three accurate samples at or above `2.8m/s` are needed before faster movement
  is considered robust, and one spike never qualifies a local transition.
- Evidence-backed same-place round trips, including unsaved endpoints within the
  `450m` local-movement band, remain valid when they contain
  at least three route samples, `1800m` of route, `650m` excursion from the
  origin, and robust faster movement. This is the narrow exception to
  distinct endpoints established by PR #114; short same-site loops are not
  commutes. Route distance and straight-line distance remain separate.
- Large-site continuity may coalesce only unknown-to-unknown stays with an
  actual visit signal, a credible later dwell, no more than `450m` endpoint
  separation, no more than `45m` transition time, no more than `1200m` local
  route distance, and at least two credible pedestrian-speed route samples.
  Never use this rule for distinct saved/learned places or vehicle evidence,
  and never enlarge the global unknown or saved-place matching radii to infer
  site identity.
- All local-day keys use the user's IANA zone, never UTC string slicing.

## Saved-place dwell and continuity

The `location-v2.0` evidence format is unchanged; this is a code-versioned correction consumed by both mobile and server. A later signed mobile build is required to ship the shared-engine change. Replay requires one device journal per invocation.

- Apply the exact `300_000ms` saved/accepted-learned floor to the effective supported window, including clipped completed Visits. Open candidates cannot accumulate attendance from the processing clock, with one exception below; midpoint uncertainty cannot create dwell from an isolated fix.
- Corroborated arrival presence: an arrival-only Visit at the active saved place (accurate, strong match) corroborated within `savedPlaceArrivalCorroborationWindowMs` by that place's `geofence_enter` or a strong accurate inside fix establishes presence. Corroboration is computed once per run and must come from the same episode: no exit for that place and no accurate reading or accurate Visit elsewhere may lie between the Visit and its corroborating evidence. It sets `arrivalPresence` on the stay the Visit joins. After that, a silence up to `savedPlaceOpenVisitPresenceMaximumMs` does not close the stay: the next observation goes through the ordinary departure rules (exit grace, two-point outside confirmation, other place), so a single outlier cannot end it. Inferred continuity stays `uncertain_gap`, at most medium. A present stay closed by departure evidence ends at the earliest credible departure (a buffered outside reading, a pending exit or the closing item), with the last inside observation as the lower bound, not the midpoint of the silence. Its dwell counts to that departure, within the cap. An open present stay counts dwell to processing time only while no exit or outside reading is unresolved.
  - Hand-over to the completed callback. iOS reports the same Visit again on departure (same device and arrival time) with a broader averaged coordinate: in a week of staging data the completion was less precise than the arrival in most pairs and beyond 65 m in four. Such companion callbacks never remove presence; one may only end it. They are paired before corroboration, so an arrival's own companions never contradict it (Visits with any other start still do). The earliest-ending companion that is spatially compatible with the place (the place is not `outside` its match), with no other Visit starting inside it, is the arrival's interval support: presence ends at its reported departure (`arrivalPresenceUntilAt`, so no gap bridging, dwell or departure claim past it), and it joins the stay when the arrival does, whatever the delivery order. An accurate one keeps the position beside the arrival it always had, so existing stay identities do not change; a broad (>65 m) one, never used before, follows the arrival, so its stay keeps its identity when it is delivered. Every other companion, including incompatible or contradicted ones, is unused: the engine runs again without it (until no new unused companion appears), so it is never a speed predecessor, contradiction, physical-stop boundary, route sample or reusable support and never moves the departure or dwell; it stays in the raw journal and counts as processed. Without a usable companion presence is as if none had arrived. Independently, a completed Visit is never the predecessor for a fix's implied speed: its coordinate averages the whole interval, not a position at its arrival time. Support resting on a broad completion stays inferred: medium confidence, never automatic. A selected companion already accepted by the corroborated broad-Visit path keeps that path and still bounds presence. With presence, the stay ends at the earliest departure evidence however late the reported departure is.
  - Without a corroborated arrival, a completed saved-place Visit is the only support. When it ends after the transition but no more than `savedPlaceVisitDepartureLagMaximumMs = 300_000` after the earliest departure evidence, the stay ends at that evidence with the last inside observation as the lower bound. This uses only Visits that arrived within the current stay (`episodeVisitUntilAt`, the latest such end), whether or not they end later than support reused from an earlier episode; reused support, which something already broke, never qualifies, and interval-support selection itself is unchanged. A Visit running further past contradicting evidence keeps the ordinary bounded estimate.
  - Point-only gaps keep the thirty-minute rule; learned places are unchanged. Evidence: a 2 Oct staging school run (a 36-minute silence moved the journey end to the next reading); a 3 Oct gym visit (the completed Visit drained 29 minutes after departure and moved the stay end to the middle of a silent hour); and a 4 Oct drop-off and 24-minute stop (a 94 m completion drained 30 minutes after departure cancelled the arrival's presence, so the stop and both journeys vanished).
- Keep a same-place exit ephemeral and pending for up to `savedPlaceExitReentryGraceMs = 300_000`. Strong inside evidence takes precedence over an uncorroborated callback. Coordinate-free re-entry requires compatible finite Visit support. Real other-place/outside corroboration still closes the episode; repeated exits are not independent outside points.
- An unsupported pending exit resolves on ordinary processing after grace using last-inside/exit bounds, capped at medium confidence. Finalisation remains ten minutes; there is no new timer or queue.
- Finite same-device compatible Visit support survives cancelled chatter. After a genuine departure, reuse is clipped to a later independently supported episode; a future Visit end is never an earlier GPS fix.
- Edge clusters and identity by centre (4 Oct evening stop). Continuity and identity are separate. Continuity: a saved stay with still accurate GPS fixes, none of them inside the place's circle (its radius, no tolerance), without a corroborated arrival or broad-Visit support, is an edge cluster (`edgeOnly`, scanned incrementally). Moving fixes (a drive through the circle) and simulated fixes never make it ordinary; one still fix inside does. The cluster begins at the stay's first Visit or non-moving reading (`edgeClusterStart`), as an unknown cluster starts: earlier movement is route evidence, and the stay is reported from there. Membership and departure are measured from the centre of the cluster (`nearEdgeCluster`). It bridges silence only as an unknown cluster would (`sparseUnknownContinuityMaximumGapMs` within `sparseUnknownContinuityMaximumDistanceMeters`; never the saved place's strong-point quiet gap, whose strong matches extend beyond the circle) and admits still readings just beyond the tolerance band within `unknownStayBaseRadiusMeters`, but only while no outside evidence is pending; moving readings beyond the band confirm departure instead, so its start and end can differ by about a minute from the unsaved case. It ends where an unknown cluster would: at any accurate, non-simulated reading beyond that radius, whatever place it matches and however fast it moves (a stop on the circle's far side, a drive through it), or at a credible accurate Visit elsewhere. Evidence that cannot move the cluster is set aside before gap and exit handling: the place's own geofence callbacks (entries, exits, registration snapshots; the cluster was never inside the circle), simulated evidence, and a displaced callback of the cluster's own Visit (a Visit with an accurate, genuine callback in the cluster, same device and arrival time; a broad or simulated callback there does not make it own). A lone Visit or geofence callback is not a cluster, and a stay described as unknown with no still fix and no Visit is movement, not a stay. Identity (`stayIdentity`, every saved stay): the accuracy-weighted centre of the stay's still accurate fixes (native speed, else implied, below `movementSpeedThresholdMps`; Visits and moving fixes never count, because the drive away can cross the circle) must lie inside the circle; otherwise the stay is unknown with the place as a candidate, promoted under unknown rules, unless iOS placed the device inside during this episode by its last still fix (`iosPlacedInside`): a non-reused Visit callback wholly inside the circle, its accuracy included, or a latest genuine geofence transition for the place, from five minutes before the stay, that is an entry. Simulated callbacks and same-place registration snapshot pairs (paired across the whole journal before the window is applied) are not crossings; an entry followed by an exit is a drive through; Visits reused from an earlier episode are interval support only. Corroborated arrivals and broad-Visit support shape timing, never identity, because their corroboration rests on matcher classes whose tolerance band extends beyond the circle. Without any still fix, only iOS signals make a stay the saved place. The working key is unchanged; a stay described as unknown carries that identity in its ID, so when a late iOS entry proves the place, replay retires the earlier segment and its open Review instead of leaving a stale proposal. Replay marks such a retirement with `review_items.ignored_scope = 'superseded'` (users ignore once or by source), keeps that segment row mutable for every replay protection check (segment persistence, protected replacements and decided boundaries), and reopens the proposal if the segment returns (for example when a later exit revokes the entry's proof); a user's decision is never reopened. An Ignore the user queued before that retirement is recorded as theirs (`ignored_scope = 'once'`) when it arrives, and a replay-retired row whose segment is absent again is superseded like any other derived row. Tests compare stays with and without the place saved (the same stays within two minutes) and port the reviewer's adversarial cases. Evidence: on 4 Oct a phone parked 123–126 m from the School pin (radius 100) recorded only plausible matches and a twelve-minute silence; the School stay restarted after the silence and the first twenty minutes became travel. Across 47 saved stays in the private corpus and the staging week the still centre never left its circle (largest 94 %, a gym visit with a geofence entry), and their output is unchanged.
- Strong same-saved-ID coordinate endpoints can bridge at most `savedPlaceQuietGapMaxMs = 1_800_000` without Visit support, only without an unresolved exit or intervening credible outside/other-place evidence. This inferred continuity stays `uncertain_gap` and at most medium. The ordinary twelve-minute route gap, unknown sixty-minute/large-site rules, learned-place gap policy and matching radii are unchanged.
- Server replay uses bounded exact lineage reads and occupied interval portions to hold ambiguous changed-ID replacements of terminal/manual sources. The server-effective `v2_review` retained-replay path may use the internal `review_scalability_v1` profile: protected candidate evidence IDs are bounded at 2,048/512 KiB and mutable lineage at 2,048 typed JSONB rows/1 MiB UTF-8, while segment lock/upsert and semantic/Review batches remain at 250. Deletion stays separate, and the existing predicates, owner scoping, deterministic ordering, row locks, conflicts and ownership triggers remain authoritative. A shared long Visit or boundary alone must not suppress a separate later episode. Obsolete open proposals become superseded snapshots atomically; expired evidence is not disappearance proof. Manual open corrections are protected too.
- A successful complete account-journal replay replaces local derived snapshots, including empty output, in the state transaction. It must not prune from an upload batch or mutate journals, uploads, Review work, receipts or another account.

## Saved-place arrival corroboration and fallback

An accepted finite same-device Visit may provide conditional temporal interval support when its horizontal accuracy is strictly above the existing `65m` high-quality cutoff and at or below the existing `200m` acceptance ceiling. The Visit must have an ordered finite departure by `processingAt`, meet the unchanged five-minute dwell floor, and match one saved place without a competing strong saved identity. Two distinct `standard_location`/`significant_change` points at that saved place must occur inside the first `300_000ms` of the Visit, and a later independent strong cluster must span the existing `300_000ms` dwell while remaining continuous and near the Visit departure. Same-place `geofence_enter` may add lineage but cannot replace the required points.

The shared helper vetoes simulated, ambiguous, competing-place, credible outside/moving, and unresolved-exit evidence. It records the actual Visit, point and callback IDs in occurrence order and carries the Visit arrival/departure intervals into a medium-confidence, `uncertain_gap` stay. The Visit coordinate is not a centre/sample/speed observation. Affected commutes carry an ephemeral inferred-boundary marker and are low confidence; no automatic-policy table, global threshold, radius, dwell, continuity, retention or rollout value changes.

If interval support fails, a narrow witness still requires two distinct strong same-place points spanning at least `120_000ms` and at most the arrival window, plus a compatible same-place geofence enter or finite Visit. It suppresses only a conflicting different-saved-destination commute that spans the witness and preserves the later stay and genuine return trip; it does not create a stay, shorten a commute to a late endpoint, or reject unrelated long travel. The analysis is replay-local and pure. Mobile complete-journal rebuild and server replay call the same shared engine; existing journal, upload, segment, Review, lineage, receipt and protection owners remain in force.

## Storage, privacy, and rollout

Mobile uses `dayframe-location-v2.db` with WAL, foreign keys, a 5s busy timeout, and evidence/outbox/account/state/segment tables. All mutations run through one rejection-safe serial queue. Seven-day cleanup expires journal rows, including unsent rows; it does not expire every raw upload body or retained context. Frozen queued/batched bodies, including acknowledged bodies containing raw coordinates, and engine/account/segment context can survive cleanup. Login and successful upload do not purge those copies. Explicit logout deletes the five owner-local stores; Delete recent evidence deletes journal/outbox/engine state, retaining account/segment context. Bounded expiry/deletion of those copies is separate privacy/storage work. Row-count cleanup may remove only acknowledged or permanently rejected rows and must never evict pending uploads.

Location follows the existing persisted `mobileAccount`, not whichever account happens to be active at callback delivery. Snapshot the backend/account-bound capture generation before the first await and revalidate it in the exclusive SQLite commit. Reject pre-binding observations and Visits whose arrival precedes binding, including straddling Visits; never fabricate or clip source times. Delete legacy unbound rows locally, never re-key them. Explicit logout stops Expo/geofence/native capture, clears the signing-out owner's caches and local journal/outbox/state/context, and warns about unsynchronised Location loss. Involuntary authentication invalidation and account replacement stop capture and clear the active binding/caches while preserving accepted, isolated prior-owner evidence under existing retention. It resumes upload only for that exact authenticated owner. Same-owner bootstrap/refresh and cold/headless restart preserve a valid generation; capture needs neither network nor a fresh Keychain read.

Opt-out invalidates the generation and retains a disabled binding/context for accepted work. Catalogue refresh is update-only for the exact live owner/binding/revision and requires scoped consent at commit; it cannot create/re-enable capture or lift suspension. Tasks, source starts and SQLite admission independently check local scoped consent. Capture cutoff and semantic cutover are separate: restore only legitimately established backend/account-scoped mode acknowledgement through involuntary invalidation, opt-out and attributable upgrade; reject ambiguous legacy state. Explicit logout resets eligibility and actual server mode changes retain their transition rules. A frozen old shadow request cannot overwrite an already valid semantic acknowledgement. The entire explicit logout is fenced against bootstrap, including without a capture binding, and stale SQLite deletion rolls back for B or a newer A session. Cleanup failures reject logout and keep admission closed until retry or session replacement. OS start/stop/native-clear/drain work shares one runtime lane; captured teardown cannot touch a replacement binding. Geofence registrations carry the capture generation, and only IDs in the current owner's catalogue are admitted. Consent and cached places/diagnostics are backend/account-specific; a legacy device flag migrates only with a demonstrable matching binding. Mobile API traffic must use the SecureStore bearer as its only session carrier and explicitly omit shared cookies; otherwise an expired background bearer can be hidden by a valid React Native cookie while evidence silently accumulates locally. HTTP `401/403` clears the rejected bearer, its matching native shortcut context, and publishes the signed-out transition only if that bearer still owns the current session; delayed responses from a replaced login must become retryable stale-session failures instead. `413` shrinks/requeues a batch, `400/422` rejects only the permanently invalid items, and retryable failures use bounded exponential backoff with jitter.

One synchronisation pass drains at most five native chunks of 100 signals and uploads at most five ready batches in order. Permanent invalid batches may be skipped so later evidence can proceed; payload resizing, retryable transport/server failure, and authentication failure stop the pass. Foreground replay bypasses the ordinary five-minute replay throttle, concurrent requests coalesce, and diagnostics store only high-level counts, status, version, and timestamps.

Server ingestion atomically creates one coordinate-free `activity_events` batch summary and stores exact evidence in user-owned `location_evidence`. Dedicated replay subsequently derives segments and semantics. Evidence APIs require both workspace and user ownership and return `private, no-store`; raw evidence is never logged. Deletion removes exact evidence and cascading lineage while preserving derived summaries and entries.

Rollout has four server-authoritative modes, returned by bootstrap and acknowledged by the mobile client:

- `v1`: deprecated explicit compatibility mode; stop and clear V2 capture and keep the previous geofence semantics. It is never a default or fallback.
- `v2_shadow`: capture/replay V2 but emit no review item or time-entry semantics. V1 classification is also suppressed, so shadow is silent rather than a semantic fallback.
- `v2_review`: suppress competing V1 location semantics and permit V2 stays/commutes that begin after the same-mode acknowledgement cutover to become review items only.
- `v2_enabled`: apply the canonical automatic logging table in `docs/PRD.md`. Normal confidence is `medium_high`/`high`; each valid boundary width is at most five minutes. Trusted stays may coexist with manual/Health time; conflicts with another location stay or commute allow at most five minutes per existing entry. Standard commutes require two saved endpoints and two route samples with significant endpoint displacement or meaningful same-place round-trip qualification. The medium exception requires distinct saved endpoints, three accepted route samples and significant endpoint displacement/route distance. Both require actual internal gap at most twelve minutes and at most five minutes per confirmed/accepted overlap. Saved-stay defaults and category-only Commute semantics remain unchanged.

The server default is `v2_shadow` through `DAYFRAME_LOCATION_ROLLOUT_MODE`. A legacy client request for `v2` maps to `v2_review`, but new code and operational documentation must use the four canonical names. The server records a semantic cutover only after a client has acknowledged the same server mode; final segments that began before that cutover cannot backfill user-visible semantics. This acknowledgement barrier is the protection against shadow-era history suddenly producing duplicate suggestions.

Location review mutations lock the review/event and affected segment rows in one transaction. Edit-and-confirm, record-once, save-place-and-confirm, split, and merge either commit fully or roll back. Change-place feedback is a small bounded anchor and never expands a saved geofence from one noisy sample.

Shared `automaticPolicy.ts` owns pure confidence, independent boundary, route and overlap decisions. Server catalogue lookups prove saved/linked/logging-enabled identity. `commute_segments.max_gap_seconds` stores the maximum internal observation gap (rounded upward to integral seconds); `gapDurationSeconds` remains total travel time. Legacy missing metrics/bounds are ineligible until evidence legitimately reconstructs them. Shared matching selects a deterministic best saved candidate and keeps up to four alternatives without radius expansion. Explicit confirmation still permits overlap; source/event/segment and mutation identity remain mandatory.

Policy rollout and replay must not rewrite an earlier user-facing decision. An
event already awaiting Review remains there even if a later deployment would
make it auto-eligible. Confirmed, accepted, and ignored events are terminal for
semantic replay. If the user deletes an automatically logged commute through
the ordinary entry editor, the confirmed source event remains as its audit
record and prevents retained-evidence replay from recreating the entry. This is
the false-positive recovery path; no commute-specific Undo surface is added.

Reverse geocoding is display-only: use saved/learned identity first, invoke a provider only for actionable/visible unknowns, cache a bounded provider/version/locale result, and never put the provider's raw object in an event. V2 segmentation must not call reverse geocoding.

Postgres retention is operational through Vercel Cron calling `GET /api/cron/location-retention` daily at `03:17 UTC`. The route fails closed unless its bearer token matches `CRON_SECRET`, returns `no-store`, calls the bounded service-role cleanup function under an advisory lock, and warns if a 50,000-row run limit leaves a backlog. Vercel Cron runs on production deployments only, so hosted verification must confirm the environment secret, database role/function grant, invocation logs, and next-day schedule. A failed invocation leaves evidence for the next run and is visible through the non-2xx route result and Vercel logs; it must never fall back to an unauthenticated deletion path.

The checked-in/default rollout remains `v2_shadow` as a fail-closed V2 mode. Missing, invalid, or stale configuration must never activate V1. Deploy the additive migration, then API/web, then the native build. Production may activate `v2_review` or the owner-approved narrow `v2_enabled` policy through the server environment only after the corresponding code is deployed. A client must acknowledge the same mode before new segments can emit semantics. Roll back within V2 by changing the server mode to `v2_shadow` or `v2_review`; do not reactivate V1. Additive tables may remain and confirmed V2 entries must not be deleted.

## Classification invariant

Location sampling is evidence, not a saved place. Classification thresholds live in `packages/shared/src/index.ts` under `LOCATION_LEARNING_THRESHOLDS`; do not duplicate them in mobile or server code.

- `noise`: weak, brief, inaccurate, or unstable evidence. Keep it local/raw so future recurrence can add evidence, but do not create a Review item or saveable learned-place row.
- `one_off_activity`: one stable stay with at least four samples and 60 minutes of dwell. Queue event-first `unknown_stay` evidence for Review; never expose Save place from this classification.
- `place_candidate`: at least two visits on two distinct days, six samples, 40 minutes cumulative dwell, a 20-minute longest stay, acceptable accuracy, and stable spread. Only this classification may upsert or promote a `learned_places` saveable candidate.

The server must re-run shared classification before an upsert. Never trust `event_type = learned_place_visit` as proof that the evidence is saveable; old mobile queues can outlive a TestFlight build.

## Evidence and persistence

Track visit count, distinct days, sample count, cumulative and longest dwell, first/last seen, average accuracy, maximum cluster spread, and radius. Cumulative mobile evidence is monotonic, so conflict updates use `greatest(...)`; do not add a cumulative payload to an already cumulative database count.

Ignored clusters stay suppressed. Noise or one-off rows may later become place candidates when new repeat evidence arrives, but direct place promotion must require `classification = 'place_candidate'`.

## Geocoding and labels

`apps/mobile/src/lib/locationGeocoding.ts` owns the provider abstraction. Expo/Apple reverse geocoding is the default and requires no external key. A future external Places/POI implementation must be optional, implement `ReverseGeocodingProvider`, document its key/cost/privacy behavior, and preserve the Expo fallback.

Resolve only when an actionable candidate is queued or a visible legacy candidate lacks cached readable data. Cache the address, POI name, formatted address, and resolution timestamp on `learned_places`; do not geocode samples or renders.

Prefer a genuine POI/business name, then street/locality/postcode. Coordinates are a final fallback and secondary debug/copy detail. Never call a one-visit cluster “regular.”

## Privacy and UX

Do not log raw coordinates or geocoder payloads. Preserve workspace/user scoping and the event-first flow. Learned places contains only saveable place candidates; one-off activity stays in Review with Confirm/Edit/Ignore. Address and coordinate copy actions must remain phone-safe and use at least 44px touch targets.

The normal mobile Review and Location Evidence presentation must translate this technical model into user language. A commute is named `Commute`, not `Possible journey`; Review cards prioritise the activity, minute-level time range, category, accessible five-step confidence, and a concise reason automatic logging did not apply. Do not duplicate a location overlap reason in a second warning row. Location Evidence shows the activity, overall time range, and map without raw DTO summaries, sample counts, technical anchor labels, retention timestamps, millisecond timestamps, or a standalone uncertainty section. A commute map exposes only an honest approximate route and explicit Start/End markers in the normal presentation; a direct endpoint fallback is dashed and labelled as approximate. Do not remove split, merge, place correction, save-place, record-once, confirm, or ignore semantics when simplifying that presentation.

Stay evidence correction separates `Where were you?`, `What did you do?`, and `When?`; commute evidence omits Where and keeps What/When because the route is not a visit place. An unmatched stay does not present absence as a large answer card. On open, every stay including a saved match makes one cancellable native `MKLocalPointsOfInterestRequest` within 750 metres and displays at most three results under `Nearby places`. When at least two distinct nearby names share a distinctive non-locality token, the same native owner may make one additional region-required `MKLocalSearch` for that context. Hard-filter the merged set to 750 metres, prefer the first matching contextual venue, then dining/activity diversity before another same-site retail tenant, and demote parking, transport, toilets, ATMs, petrol, and similar utilities to fallback positions. Preserve Apple order within a semantic group, using distance and name only as deterministic tie-breakers. Apple exposes no public popularity or containing-venue score, so call results nearby, never popular, likely, or inferred. Context/category/address/ranking data stays transient and must not cross the persistence boundary. A two-character typed query replaces that list through the existing MapKit completion search; clearing restores the already loaded nearby results. Do not fetch proactive POIs for commutes. Keep the baseline saved match first and selected, followed by other saved choices and nearby Apple choices. Deduplicate a normalised name at the same immediate point (25 m presentation-only tolerance); preserve other same-name venues. Cancel stale work on route/account departure; Apple never participates in automatic matching.

Selecting a POI updates only the transient visible proposal and defaults `Save for future visits` off. `Use once and record` sends `record_poi_once` with the trimmed name and optional entry edits; the derived `time_entries.place_label` stores that name only and must be mutually exclusive with `place_id`. Never persist Apple identifiers, addresses, POI coordinates, or raw result payloads for a one-time location. `Save place and record` retains the existing atomic saved-place transaction and bounded learning feedback. Ordinary description/category/time edits preserve `place_label`; explicit saved-place selection clears it. Read models expose `placeName` and `placeKind`, and reports group one-time labels by case-insensitive whitespace-normalized name. Offline/failure keeps typed search, map pin, and unknown-place recording available. Saved-place correction plus entry edits still commit through one transaction. Commute endpoint correction is not inferred from stay-place semantics. Review uses only existing categories and display-only glyphs until a shared persisted icon contract is approved.

## Upload and replay reliability

Diagnose capture, evidence upload and retained-evidence replay separately. An
empty upload queue does not prove processing succeeded. The existing metadata
owner retains bounded backend/account-bound endpoint results, safe request IDs,
transaction phase/substage and separate server/client durations. Never retain
arbitrary exception text, SQL, bodies, coordinates, places or source IDs in these
records. Success clears only its endpoint's failure; observation cannot cause
redelivery. Auth/stale/cancelled responses cannot update replacement-owner state.

Upload uses one parameterised multi-row insert after the coordinate-free summary;
acknowledge conflict-skipped and policy-rejected IDs only after commit. Preserve
first-input persistence, classification and expiry. Measured replay lineage uses
250-row chunks, retaining protected/manual/terminal links and one atomic delete
plus replacement. Do not broaden replay scope or increase timeouts when another
stage exceeds the budget. Use the guarded `validate:location-reliability` runner
for substantial synthetic seven-day output and finite 555 + seven/305 backlog
passes. Synthetic latency is not hosted evidence. Missing return observations
remain a separate capture investigation; successful upload cannot reconstruct them.
The transaction owner may skip only a known identical 3,000 ms/1,500 ms full-cap
guard pair for server-effective `v2_review` retained replay. A lower remaining
budget, savepoint recovery, timeout-setting SQL or any uncertain state falls back
to the existing guard query; ingest, `v2_shadow`, `v2_enabled` and non-Location
callers remain unchanged.

## Strong-evidence short-journey exception

`commute.ts` retains the ordinary three-minute rule except for positive shorter intervals between existing, distinct eligible stays at least `commuteMinimumEndpointDistanceMeters` apart. Three independent route observations must meet existing 65m/2.8m/s thresholds. Only GPS point kinds with normalised `isSimulated: false` qualify. [Expo Location's `mocked` field is Android-only](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/sdk/location/#locationobject); on iOS the absent field is normalised to `false` by Dayframe capture, so `false` is not independent proof that an Expo sample was not simulated. Native callbacks that omit `isSimulated`, including the current significant-change bridge, still fail closed for this exception. Simulated iOS/GPX/Xcode input is not reliably rejected by this field. Neither Visit/callback anchors nor broad or invalid samples count.

Preprocessing deduplicates `(deviceId, clientEvidenceId)` and normalises negative native speed to absent; its implied-speed >120m/s rejection applies only to standard points. The exception additionally rejects implausible supplied or implied speed for every source. Implied speed requires the actual preceding coordinate observation to be an accurate GPS point with `isSimulated: false` on the same device, subject to the iOS provenance limit above. A supplied speed cannot mask implausible implied movement.

Exception proof uses existing device/ID and parsed source-time fields, plus conservative exact device/coordinate deduplication across sources. The latter prevents native second-resolution mirrors of Expo millisecond samples from adding proof without introducing time buckets or rewriting source IDs. Repeated identical coordinates do not add independent proof even at different times. Source and occurrence times must lie strictly inside the candidate window. Original route lineage and summary remain intact; proof counting does not mutate evidence, confidence, boundaries or stable endpoint-based identities. Timestamp precomputation remains invocation-local and proof analysis is lazy and reused.

Shared `automaticPolicy.ts` checks actual start/stop instants, not rounded duration seconds, and reports `short_journey_review_only` below three minutes. Mobile translates that reason explicitly. Ordinary automatic eligibility is unchanged. Mode/cutover, finalisation, arrival witnesses, retention and persistence protection owners are unchanged. Newly eligible retained evidence after cutover can produce a proposal on an ordinary later replay; this does not authorise live replay or historical repair.

## Physical stops and trips with stops

`physicalStops.ts` decides that the device stopped without using place identity, dwell gates or Review eligibility. Only accepted standard/significant-change fixes with accuracy at most 65 m and `isSimulated !== true` can support a stop. A broader accepted fix never joins a stop, but it breaks a cluster, or counts as departure, when its whole accuracy disc lies beyond the stop radius (or 150 m), so accepted movement cannot be bridged into a stationary interval. A credible Visit elsewhere (accuracy disc beyond 150 m) is never a slow fix, but it splits a cluster whose time it falls within and bounds a stop's arrival or departure. A Visit is elsewhere only when every callback iOS sent for it (same device and arrival time) is: the completed callback averages the whole Visit and can sit far from the stop its own arrival callback places. Mirrors at the same second, or at the same coordinate within five seconds, count once, preferring the copy with native speed. A cluster starts at a slow fix (below 1.5 m/s); only accurate fixes within `physicalStopRadiusMeters` of its slow-fix centre join it. It ends only where departure is evident (vehicle speed of at least 2.8 m/s, or a fix certainly beyond the 150 m departure distance) or after a gap over the sparse-continuity limit, so nearby slow fixes cannot form a second cluster that reuses the same boundaries; overlapping stops are never emitted. Movement (vehicle speed or 150 m displacement) must be observed within `physicalStopBoundaryWindowMs` on both sides; unbounded clusters fail closed.

Corroboration requires a completed overlapping Visit plus at least two slow fixes spread over `physicalStopMinimumSlowSpreadMs`, or without a Visit at least three slow fixes spread over `physicalStopUnanchoredMinimumSlowSpreadMs`. Observed movement bounds both ends; a Visit estimate is only clamped inside them. Owner-labelled 30–60 second kerbside stops produced 5–7 minute Visits and two fixes one second apart, so never let a Visit or near-simultaneous fixes establish a stop alone. Stops shorter than `physicalStopMinimumDurationMs` are ignored.

A corroborated stop that covers promoted stays (each within `physicalStopAbsorbToleranceMs` of its bounds) and extends them by at least `physicalStopAbsorbExtensionMs` replaces them: place logic split or shortened one physical stop at a silence, an exit or edge matches (the capture simulator showed a drive through a saved circle before parking beside it, where the pending exit swallowed the first still fix and the stay restarted after the silence). The replacement takes the union of the stop and its fragments, the place only when every fragment was that saved place and `stillAtSavedPlace` holds for the merged evidence, and otherwise unknown identity with all their candidates. A replacement at the saved place starts no earlier than its first accurate observation at the place, in its circle or tolerance band (the stop's own start can be an estimate between the last fix away and the first one there, and readings beyond the band are not attendance), so saved attendance never begins before the observed arrival; its ID includes the place, so it differs from the unknown one over the same evidence (and from another place's), and a late iOS entry that proves the place makes replay retire the unknown segment and its open Review. No silence reaching time it newly claims (before, after or between its fragments) may be longer than `savedPlaceQuietGapMaxMs`, measured in full between accurate, genuine location fixes (never geofence callbacks, broad or simulated readings, nor a fragment's estimated end) unless one of the episode's own qualified Visits spans it (a genuine one that arrived within this physical stop, never simulated or an earlier episode's, and that the saved-place rules corroborated for that place, over its corroborated interval, or an accurate completed Visit at the place; for an unknown replacement, within its radius of the stop), so the saved-place arrival-boundary contract (no presence across unsupported silence) holds. A commute into a replacement (or into stays coalesced into it) keeps the low confidence an absorbed stay's inferred boundary gave it. Across the private corpus and the staging week, stops and promoted stays otherwise agree within about three minutes and no output changes. A corroborated stop not overlapping a promoted stay becomes an ordinary unknown stay with `formation: "physical_stop"` (persisted in stay metadata) and nearby saved/learned places only as `candidatePlaceIds`. `assembleTripsThroughStops` derives each trip directly between the stays either side of unknown stays shorter than `unknownStayReviewDwellMs`, treating those stops as observed route waypoints: their evidence is not route evidence, and their interval is subtracted from observation gaps in the engine and in Review evidence, while real unobserved route gaps beside a stop are still reported. Every portion of the trip (into the first stop, between stops, out of the last) must contain an accurate movement sample (at least 1.5 m/s, or 150 m from both ends of that portion); otherwise the legs are kept, so unobserved time beside a stop is never claimed as travel. The whole trip is qualified, so a nearby round trip whose legs would not qualify alone is kept. Conversely, when a complete chain of already-qualified legs joins the trip's endpoints through its stops, the trip qualifies even below the same-place route minimum (lowest leg confidence). Otherwise any qualified legs remain. The trip carries coordinate-free `stops` in commute metadata, keeps the endpoint-based ID of the former round trip, and holds its qualified `legs` in memory only: when protected history blocks the trip, its endpoints or any of its interior stop stays during replay, or a terminal/manual row keeps an interior stop's ID (its persisted boundaries remain canonical, and fallback legs touching it are re-derived with `deriveCommutes` inside those boundaries, rebuilding evidence, metrics and qualification including short-journey proof; legs that no longer qualify are omitted), the trip is not persisted and only legs whose own endpoints are unblocked are persisted instead, so a trip never spans decided time and unaffected open Reviews are not retired without replacement. Persisted boundaries are canonical at every commute endpoint too: replay reads them for each commute's endpoint stays, re-derives a standalone commute the same way when a decided endpoint's attaching side has moved, and falls back to re-derived legs for a trip whose decided outer endpoint has moved. Trips are identified by their `stops`, never by the presence of fallback legs: a trip whose legs do not qualify alone is omitted rather than rewritten as a stopless commute. Unmoved decided endpoints leave commutes untouched. Review evidence removes recorded stop intervals before reporting evidence gaps or split points. Saved/learned stops and visit-length unknown stops still split journeys. `automaticPolicy.ts` rejects trips with stops as `journey_contains_stop`. Do not bump `algorithmVersion` for engine behaviour changes: it keys evidence and segment rows.
